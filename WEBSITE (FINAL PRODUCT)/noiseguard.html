Latent-space defense system

Protect your images
before they're stolen from you.

NoiseGuard encodes an image into latent space, measures exactly how it can be reconstructed or mined, then injects a calibrated adversarial perturbation through PGD and MIRAGE — so the picture still looks like yours, but breaks in the hands of anything trying to model it.

Process

Four stages, one continuous pass

Every image moves through the same deterministic sequence — nothing about the process changes between the desktop app, the API, and this console.

01 — ENCODE

Into latent space

The image is passed through the VAE encoder, producing a compact latent representation and a decoded reconstruction to compare against.

VAE
02 — ANALYZE

Measure exposure

Latent mean, standard deviation, and reconstruction error quantify how easily the image could be modeled or regenerated by an outside system.

Metrics
03 — PROTECT

Constrained perturbation

PGD iteratively pushes pixels against the loss surface, then clamps the result so the perturbation stays bounded and visually unobtrusive.

PGD · N iters
04 — MIRAGE

Final pass

The protected image runs through MIRAGE for a second representation-level pass, producing the image you actually publish or share.

MIRAGE
