AI IMAGE PROTECTION · RESEARCH PROTOTYPE

Protect images from AI reconstruction.

NoiseGuard explores adversarial image perturbations that aim to alter learned representations while keeping the visible image close to the original.

Try NoiseGuard → Explore the approach

Stable Diffusion VAE encoder/decoder · PGD · MIRAGE surrogate

THE IDEA

Change the representation without visibly changing the image.

The prototype uses a frozen VAE to work with image representations, then applies two optimization stages before decoding the protected image.

HOW IT WORKS

A small research pipeline with measurable outputs.

01

Latent representation

The input is encoded with the VAE encoder and represented in latent space.

02

Bounded optimization

PGD and MIRAGE optimize the image while keeping perturbations within the configured budget.

03

Verification

The tool returns the protected image, reconstruction, latent statistics, and optimization loss information.
